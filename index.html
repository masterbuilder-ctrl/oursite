About

Fewer, better homes.

As a boutique, family-run design and building company, Jace Space is committed to delivering bespoke, high-quality builds that favour craftsmanship, character and personal connection over mass production.

With a focus on early collaboration and end-to-end delivery, we champion simplicity, honesty and adaptability — working closely with clients from the moment they purchase land through to finish selections and handover.

As Jace Space evolves, the purpose remains: to build fewer, better homes that speak to a more thoughtful, design-literate market.

Our Story
01

Communication

Honest, intuitive, and human.

Jace Space is open book by nature — clear on process, transparent on pricing, and ready to adapt. Clients are kept in the loop at every stage and welcomed into the project as collaborators, not just customers. It's about building trust as much as building homes.

02

Reputation

Built on integrity.

Jace Space was founded in honour of family, and its name continues to carry that same care and pride. Reputation is earned through consistency, honesty, and going the extra millimetre — even when no one's looking.

03

Culture

A team worth building with.

Jace Space nurtures a hands-on, down-to-earth team culture where everyone's voice matters and every skill is sharpened. Clients feel it too — in the friendly energy on site, the shared pride in the work, and the sense that you're part of something personal.

04

Detail

No shortcuts.

Every material, join, and finish is chosen and executed with intent. Jace Space homes reflect deep respect for craft — balancing style with substance, and precision with heart.

How We Work

Every Jace Space project is delivered through a considered, collaborative process — from first conversation to final handover.

Approach

Three disciplines, one studio

Ideally, we manage design and construction in-house — one point of accountability, start to finish. But we work just as readily on a single discipline, partnering with your own architect, designer or builder when that's what the project calls for.

Design only. Build only. Or both, under one roof. We work with homeowners, architects, designers and other builders to deliver considered residential projects across the Gold Coast and beyond.

01

Design

Considered architecture and interior design tailored to site, climate and how our clients actually live — available as a standalone service, or paired with our own build team.

02

Construct

Licensed, in-house construction teams delivering to the same standard as the drawings — no compromises. Just as ready to build from your architect's plans as our own.

03

Deliver

Transparent project management from council approval through to keys-in-hand handover — whether we're running the whole project or just our piece of it.
